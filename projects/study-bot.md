## What was the project brief?

This is a personal project with the goal of designing and constructing a small drone using an ESP32 microcontroller as the guidance control unit (GCU). 

This is a group project that I am completing with my friends. 

## What was my approach?

Throughout my time at university I've developed a passion in control engineering and robotics. In addition, I've always had an interest in flight control systems and learning how vehicles such as planes, helicopters, and rockets know which way to point. 

Based on this interest, I've taken on the responsibility of assembling the GCU for the drone - a combination of the microcontroller (ESP32), and IMU (MPU6500 breakout board). Additionally, I've added a small OLED screen for troubleshooting guidance code during the testing phase of our project.
